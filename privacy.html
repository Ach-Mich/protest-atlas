What this site stores

  • No cookies and no tracking or analytics.
  • Your browser stores your language choice (a setting called pa-lang) in local storage so the site opens in the language you picked. It never leaves your device and can be cleared in your browser.

What happens when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, it receives your IP address and technical request data and may keep server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Third-party resources. To display the page, your browser currently loads fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) and map libraries from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These providers receive your IP address and browser details. We intend to host these files ourselves; when we do, this section will be updated.
  • Source links. Links to news sites and other sources take you to third-party sites with their own privacy rules.
