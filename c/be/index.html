Belgium

Last checked Friday 27 November at 06:52.

Unions FGTB, CSC and Synova have protested all year against the De Wever government's austerity plan; about 50,000 marched in Brussels on 23 November by the police count (70,000 per unions) as the coalition remained deadlocked on the budget. Students are also protesting cuts to education.

Why they protest: Wage indexation; Pensions; Spending cuts; VAT increase; Working time.

Next protest, Thursday 10 December. The union common front of the non-profit sector (social, health and culture, public and private) calls a national demonstration in Brussels on Thursday 10 December, starting at Brussels-North; no strike notice reported. At skeyes (air traffic control), the CSC Transcom and SLFP unions have filed a strike notice from 9 December; no strike date set (RTBF).

Who is protesting, and what they want

FGTB (socialist union) All year
Keep automatic wage indexation · No VAT increase · No longer working time

CSC (Christian union) All year
Oppose the government's socio-economic policy · Scrap the pension malus

Synova (union) Since 21 October
No budget savings at the expense of workers, benefit recipients and public services

French-speaking students Autumn 2026
No cuts to education budgets

Pressure on government

Demand
Change of programme. Unions oppose the government's austerity plan
Response
No response yet
Government
Strained. Arizona coalition deadlocked on the budget; the King asked PM De Wever to keep negotiating, with talks extended to the new year

Sources: Brussels Times, L'Avenir, 23 Nov 2026, La Libre (Belga), 23 Nov 2026

Protest days

National demonstration in Brussels

Monday 23 November. 50,000 official · 70,000 organisers · 8 arrests

A company vehicle was set on fire near Gare du Midi; police used tear gas and water cannon against a group of about 200 that police said was separate from the march. All departing flights cancelled; journalists' association AJP reported a press photographer struck by an officer.

Sources: L'Avenir, 23 Nov 2026, RTBF, 23 Nov 2026 (police and union counts, arrests), RTBF live, 23 Nov 2026

Student march in Brussels against education cuts

Tuesday 17 November. Turnout not reported

Thousands per Paris Match; after the march protesters blocked the inner ring road between Madou and Arts-Loi, and police stopped them reaching the Les Engagés headquarters. No arrests reported.

Sources: Paris Match Belgique, 17 Nov 2026, CSC, call for 17 Nov
