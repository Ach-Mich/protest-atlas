Protests in AlbaniaAlbania

Last checked Friday 27 November at 06:49.

Student-led protests since 13 October, joined by the opposition Democratic Party, demand Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation over corruption, education costs and poor job prospects; clashes with police recur each week.

Why they protest: Corruption; Education costs; Jobs.

Democratic Party (opposition, Sali Berisha) Since 24 October
