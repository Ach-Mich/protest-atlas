Portugal

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:29.

Unions are protesting the 2027 budget over pay, pensions and public services: public administration workers struck on 20 November, with about 90% adhesion in schools by the unions' count, and CP train drivers began a five-day strike on 23 November. Housing movements held a national day of action on 14 November; the CGTP marched in Lisbon on 21 November.

Why they protest: Wages; Public services; Cost of living; Housing.

Next protest, Wednesday 25 November. The SMAQ train drivers' strike at CP continues with a second full stoppage on 25 November and runs until 27 November. The CGTP had called a rally outside parliament for the final 2027 budget vote on 24 November; no report of it found yet. No later national date announced in our sources yet.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Casa para Viver & Porta a Porta Since 10 October
Reverse the new lease law · No evictions without rehousing · Curb purchases by property funds · More public housing

CGTP (union confederation) Since 17 October
Higher wages and pensions · Right to health, housing and education · Lower and control essential prices

Frente Comum (public administration unions) Strike on 20 November
Pay rise of at least 15%, minimum €150 · Public-sector base pay of €1,150 · Negotiate the 83 proposals

SMAQ (train drivers' union) 23 November
Recover purchasing power · Better working conditions · Stronger rail safety

Protest days

Train drivers' strike at CP begins (23-27 November)

Monday 23 November. Turnout not reported

242 of 1,062 scheduled trains (22.8%) cancelled by 19:00 on the first day per CP. On 24 November, the first full-stoppage day, 795 of 1,062 trains (74.9%) were cancelled by 19:00 per CP; only the 267 minimum-service trains ran. Full stoppage continues on 25 November.

Sources: Público, 23 Nov 2026, Observador, 23 Nov 2026, Jornal Económico, 24 Nov 2026, RTP, 24 Nov 2026

CGTP national demonstration in Lisbon

Saturday 21 November. Turnout not reported

Several thousand marched in heavy rain from Saldanha to Restauradores (Lusa); 'tens of thousands' per CGTP. No official count.

Sources: Diário de Coimbra (Lusa), 21 Nov 2026, CGTP-IN, 21 Nov 2026

National public administration strike

Friday 20 November. Turnout not reported

About 90% in schools and 100% at some hospitals per Frente Comum; about 75% of doctors per FNAM. Schools closed in several districts, waste collection stopped in several municipalities, and monuments including the Jerónimos Monastery closed. No official national figure.

Sources: Público, 20 Nov 2026, Esquerda.net, 20 Nov 2026

National Day of Struggle for Housing

Saturday 14 November. Turnout not reported

Decentralised actions across the country, including Lisbon (Alameda) and Porto (Praça do Marquês) at 15:00, the Azores and Madeira.

Sources: RTP, Nov 2026
