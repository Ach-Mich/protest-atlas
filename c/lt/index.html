Lithuania

Last checked Friday 27 November at 06:49.

The LŠDPS teachers' union began an indefinite national strike on 16 November, demanding at least a 10% pay rise from 2027; the draft budget offers 7.56%.

Why they protest: Teachers' pay.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Lithuanian Education Workers' Trade Union (LŠDPS) Since 16 November
Raise teachers' pay coefficients by at least 10% from 2027 · Average teacher pay at 130% of the national average

Pressure on government

Demand
Policy change. Teachers' pay rise of at least 10%
Response
Talks offered. PM met the union on 24 November; any rise only within the ministry's existing budget, no deal yet

Sources: Alkas.lt, 16 Nov 2026, LRT, 23 Nov 2026, LRT, 24 Nov 2026

Protest days

Indefinite national teachers' strike begins

Monday 16 November. 4,000 organisers

LŠDPS says about 4,000 education workers in 222 institutions joined; 14 more schools joined on 23 November (LRT). Other major education unions did not join. On 24 November PM Mindaugas Sinkevičius met the union and said he could not promise higher pay beyond the ministry's existing budget; the strike continues (about 3,000 teachers per LRT).

Sources: Alkas.lt, 16 Nov 2026, LRT, 6 Nov 2026 (strike call), LRT, 23 Nov 2026, LRT, 24 Nov 2026
