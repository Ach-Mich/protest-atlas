Germany

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:32.

Youth-led protests against a return of military conscription and for education, affordable living and climate protection: "Gen Z regelt" rallies in more than 30 cities on 31 October, and a fifth nationwide school strike and youth protests on 20 November, about 90,000 by the organisers' count. A Berlin march on 14 November ended with 20 detentions.

Why they protest: Education; Cost of living; Military conscription; Climate; Health.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Schulstreik gegen Wehrpflicht (school students' alliance) Autumn 2026
Stop military screenings · Repeal the military service modernisation law

"Gen Z regelt" (youth initiative) Since 31 October
Better education opportunities · Affordable living costs · More political participation for young people

Pressure on government

Demand
Policy change. Stop the return of military service
Response
No response yet

Sources: junge Welt, 2 Nov 2026, Berliner Zeitung, 20 Nov 2026

Protest days

Nationwide school strike and youth protests against conscription

Friday 20 November. 90,000 organisers

Police counted about 4,000 in Berlin (up to 15,000 per organisers), up to 4,000 in Hamburg and about 2,500 in Leipzig; marches also in Bremen, Frankfurt, Cologne, Erfurt, Potsdam, Magdeburg and Freiburg. "Gen Z regelt" reported about 90,000 nationwide. Fireworks thrown at police in Leipzig (water cannon used; one officer injured) and Hamburg.

Sources: WEB.DE (dpa), 21 Nov 2026, Berliner Zeitung, 20 Nov 2026, ZDF heute, 21 Nov 2026

"Gen Z regelt" march in Berlin

Saturday 14 November. 1,200 official · 20 arrests

About 1,200 per police against 100 registered; 700 officers deployed; pyrotechnics set off.

Sources: Volksstimme (dpa), 15 Nov 2026

"Gen Z regelt" youth rallies

Saturday 31 October. Turnout not reported

Rallies in more than 30 cities; tens of thousands per organisers, no official count. Clashes and water cannon in Frankfurt, Cologne and Hamburg.

Sources: AP via ABC News, 31 Oct 2026
