Slovakia

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:29.

Opposition parties led by Progressive Slovakia rallied on 17 November, the Velvet Revolution anniversary, in Bratislava and dozens of other towns against the Fico government, angered by the foreign minister's trip to Moscow to renew economic ties and by what they call curbs on freedoms.

Why they protest: Opposition to the Fico government; Ties with Russia; Rule of law & freedoms.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Progressive Slovakia (PS) and other opposition parties 17 November 2026
Unite the opposition to defeat the Fico government · Against renewed economic ties with Russia

Pressure on government

Demand
Change of programme. Oppose the Fico government's direction (Russia ties, freedoms)
Response
No response yet. The cabinet discussed the foreign minister's Moscow visit hours before the rallies; no response to the protests reported

Sources: AP via WSB-TV, 17 Nov 2026, noviny.sk (TV JOJ), 17 Nov 2026

Protest days

"17 November for Change" opposition rallies on the Velvet Revolution anniversary

About 25,000 on SNP Square in Bratislava per organisers (the same estimate reported by TV JOJ); AP reported protests in dozens of communities across the country. No incidents reported.
