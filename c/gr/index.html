Protests in GreeceGreece

Last checked Friday 27 November at 06:49.

Public-sector workers held a 24-hour nationwide strike on 25 November, called by ADEDY with hospital, teachers' and seafarers' unions, over pay, the 13th and 14th salaries, living costs and housing, with rallies in more than 60 cities. Students and teachers had protested over education funding and rents in October and November.

Why they protest: Wages; Cost of living; Housing & rents; Public-sector staffing; Education funding.

Protest days

24-hour nationwide public-sector strike with rallies in 60+ cities

Wednesday 25 November. Turnout not reported

Thousands rallied at Klafthmonos Square and the Propylaia in Athens and marched to parliament; rallies in more than 60 cities including Thessaloniki, Patras, Heraklion and Larissa. Metro, electric railway and tram ran only 09:00-18:00; ferries disrupted; flights affected at 15 regional airports. No incidents reported; no official or organisers' count.

Sources: athens24.gr, 25 Nov 2026, iEidiseis, 25 Nov 2026, ADEDY (strike call and rally posts)
