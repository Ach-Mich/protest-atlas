Greece

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:32.

Students camped in Syntagma Square and joined teachers, public sector workers and hospital doctors in strikes and protests in more than 50 towns on 21 October over education funding, rents and pay; students and teachers rallied again on 5 November, and ADEDY has called a public-sector strike for 25 November.

Why they protest: Education funding; Housing & rents; Wages; Hospital staffing.

Next protest, Wednesday 25 November. ADEDY's 24-hour nationwide public-sector strike on Wednesday 25 November is joined by hospital staff, teachers, seafarers (no ferries from Attica ports), rail and metro staff and private-sector labour centres; the central rally is at Propylaia in Athens at 10:30, with rallies in more than 60 cities (ERT). Demands include pay rises, the 13th and 14th salaries and action on living costs and housing.

Who is protesting, and what they want
