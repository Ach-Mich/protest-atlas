Austria

Last checked Friday 27 November at 06:49.

Students and university staff marched in Vienna on 22 October for more university funding, and young people rallied in 14 cities on 20 November against rearmament and for education and social spending; police detained dozens in Vienna.

Why they protest: University funding; Military spending; Education.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Students' union (ÖH) and university staff Since spring 2026
At least €18 billion for universities in 2028–2030

Youth strike alliance (ÖH, Fridays for Future, Attac and others) 20 November
More money for education, climate and social spending · Less for rearmament

Pressure on government

Demand
Change of programme. University funding and budget priorities (education over rearmament)
Response
No response yet

Sources: vol.at / APA, 22 Oct 2026, ORF Wien, 20 Nov 2026

Demonstration for university funding

Thursday 22 October. 30,000 organisers

About 30,000 per ÖH shortly after the start (APA); other reports cite 40,000 per organisers and up to 50,000. No police count. Police called it peaceful; six masked people held near the Ring.
