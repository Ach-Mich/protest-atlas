Croatia

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:29.

School and university students boycotted classes and gathered in about a dozen cities on 20 November over living costs, housing, education and conscription. A civic initiative has called anti-corruption protests in several cities for 12 December.

Why they protest: Cost of living; Housing; Corruption; Education & health.

Next protest, Saturday 12 December. The civic initiative "Vrijeme za promjene" has moved its anti-corruption protests in several cities (including Zagreb, Split, Rijeka, Osijek, Zadar and Pula) from 21 November to Saturday 12 December, citing safety and higher-than-expected interest; the youth strike organisers say they will continue.

Who is protesting, and what they want

Štrajk mladih u Hrvatskoj (Youth Strike in Croatia) 20 November
Lower living costs and affordable housing · Better education and healthcare · Demilitarisation, no conscription

Vrijeme za promjene (civic initiative) 12 December call
Fight corruption and abuse of power · Transparent public spending · Accountable institutions

Pressure on government

Demand
Change of programme. Living costs, housing and services; anti-corruption protests called
Response
No response yet. The education minister dismissed the strike as not spontaneous

Sources: Index.hr, 20 Nov 2026, N1 Hrvatska, 31 Oct 2026
