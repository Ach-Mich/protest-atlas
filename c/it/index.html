Italy

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:32.

The CGIL union is mobilising against the Meloni government's budget law over wages, pensions and public services; its general strike on 20 November drew about 1.5 million people in more than 100 cities by the union's count. Grassroots unions strike again on 4 December.

Why they protest: Wages & pensions; Cost of living; Budget law; Military spending.

Next protest, Friday 4 December. Grassroots unions (CUB, SGB, SI Cobas, Unicobas, ADL Varese, SBM, SGC) hold a 24-hour general strike across public and private sectors on Friday 4 December. The CGIL will join a health-sector demonstration in Rome on 5 December and holds a demonstration for the South in Cosenza on 12 December (Il Sole 24 Ore).

Who is protesting, and what they want

CGIL (union) Since 17 October
Higher wages and pensions · More for health, schools and housing · 1% wealth tax above €2 million · Less military spending

Grassroots unions (CUB, SGB, SI Cobas, Unicobas and others) 4 December strike call
Pay rises of at least 20% · Minimum wage of €12 an hour · Wage indexation to prices · Less military spending

Pressure on government

Demand
Change of programme. CGIL and grassroots unions oppose the budget law
Response
No response yet

Protest days

CGIL general strike with marches in more than 100 cities

Friday 20 November. 1,500,000 organisers

About 1.5 million in more than 100 squares and 60% average strike adhesion per CGIL; about 20,000 students in Milan, about 7,000 in Genoa, 6,000-8,000 in Bari per organisers. Police used water cannon and tear gas in Turin; firecrackers thrown at the education ministry in Rome. PD leader Elly Schlein joined the march.

Sources: ANSA, 20 Nov 2026, Fanpage live, 20 Nov 2026
