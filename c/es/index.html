Spain

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:29.

A 24-hour general strike on 11 November, called by CCOO, UGT, smaller unions and tenants' unions over rents and wages, drew 120,000 marchers in Madrid by the official count, 18 days before the 29 November general election. Rail unions struck nationwide on 17 November over safety after the 28 October Urda crash, which killed 69 people.

Why they protest: Housing; Wages; Rail safety after the Urda crash; Labour rights.

Protest days

24-hour rail strike over safety across the network

Tuesday 17 November. Turnout not reported

Madrid Cercanías almost completely halted and Media Distancia nearly suppressed; morning AVE cancellations to Barcelona, Pamplona and Castellón; Ouigo ran 17 of 27 trains and Iryo kept 70% minimum services. The Transport Ministry set minimum services of 50–75% on Cercanías and 72% on AVE and long distance.

Sources: eldiario.es, 17 Nov 2026, Qué!, 3 Nov 2026, Transport Ministry minimum-services resolution, 17 Nov 2026

24-hour general strike over housing and wages, with marches in about 100 cities

Wednesday 11 November. 120,000 official · 72 arrests

Madrid march 120,000 per the Government Delegation (400,000 to 600,000 per the tenants' union, depending on the report); Barcelona 55,000 per the Guardia Urbana (400,000 per organisers); Granada 25,000 and Málaga 14,000 per the Government Delegation. Participation disputed: unions report 41% to 75% by region, employers' group CEOE under 5%. Unrest at the end of the Barcelona march.

Sources: elDiario.es live blog, 11 Nov 2026, Qué!, 11 Nov 2026 (Interior provisional: 53 arrests, 1,044 incidents; Madrid 120,000, Barcelona 55,000), Diario Sabemos, 11 Nov 2026, ANSA, 11 Nov 2026 (midday: 40+ detentions per Interior, Ertzaintza and Mossos via Europa Press)

Protest at the Transport Ministry after the Urda crash

Wednesday 28 October. 1,000 official

More than 1,000 per officials, about 1,500 per RTVE. Riot police used batons and tear gas after flares were thrown.

Sources: Qazinform / EFE, Ground News

Housing protest in Barcelona turns violent

Monday 5 October. Turnout not reported

Fires and arrests reported; no figures in our sources

Sources: Euronews (ES), 6 Oct 2026

Housing protests in Madrid and about 50 other Spanish cities

Saturday 3 October. 70,000 official · 500,000 organisers

Tens of thousands in Madrid; peaceful in about 50 cities. In Valencia, police fired rubber bullets and tear gas and about 30 people entered the City of Arts and Sciences before being removed; protests in Barcelona and L'Hospitalet were called off because of rain. Demonstrators called for a general strike. Media reported the PM might call a snap election for November

Sources: Euronews (ES), 3 Oct 2026, El Mercurio, 3 Oct 2026, Reuters (via Cyprus Mail), 3 Oct 2026
