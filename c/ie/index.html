Ireland

Last checked Tuesday 24 November at 22:29.

Public sector unions and the government reached a draft pay deal at the Workplace Relations Commission, and the second 24-hour national strike planned for 12 November was suspended; members vote on the deal until early December. More than 300,000 workers had struck on 14 October.
