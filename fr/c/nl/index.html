Manifestations aux Pays-BasPays-Bas

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:54.

Regional bus and train drivers struck nationwide on 23 and 24 November over their collective agreement, and FNV and CNV have called more strike days in December. Earlier, a "Gen Z" youth protest in The Hague turned chaotic on 31 October and ANWB staff struck on 9 November.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Working conditions in public transport; Collective agreements.

Prochaine mobilisation, jeudi 3 décembre. FNV and CNV call further nationwide strikes in regional public transport on 3, 4, 14, 15 and 18 December, after a two-day strike on 23 and 24 November.

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

FNV and CNV (regional public transport workers) Strikes since October
Reject the employers' final offer · Workable schedules with enough recovery time · Keep protections for older drivers · Keep the irregular-hours allowance

Pression sur le gouvernement

Revendication
Changement de politique. A new collective agreement for regional public transport
Réponse
Pas encore de réponse. No new offer reported

Sources : Omroep Brabant, 23 Nov 2026, FNV, 18 Nov 2026

Journées de mobilisation

Two-day nationwide strike in regional public transport

lundi 23 novembre. Participation non communiquée

48-hour strike on 23 and 24 November by bus and regional train drivers at Arriva, Transdev, EBS, Qbuzz and Keolis, covering more than 13,000 workers under the agreement; city transport in Amsterdam, The Hague and Rotterdam and NS trains not included. Rides cancelled; no turnout figure.

Sources : Omroep Brabant, 23 Nov 2026, Treinreiziger.nl, 18 Nov 2026, NL Times (strike campaign), 9 Oct 2026

ANWB staff strike and union rally in The Hague

lundi 9 novembre. Participation non communiquée

12-hour strike at roadside assistance company ANWB with a rally at the Koekamp; no headcount reported

Sources : Rodi (The Hague), 9 Nov 2026

"Gen Z" youth protest in The Hague

samedi 31 octobre. 34 interpellations

Riot police intervened on the Malieveld; no headcount. Most of those arrested were 15 to 18 years old, per police.

Sources : NL Times, 31 Oct 2026, Rodi (The Hague), 31 Oct 2026
