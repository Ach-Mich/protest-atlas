Manifestations au PortugalPortugal

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:49.

Unions are protesting the 2027 budget over pay, pensions and public services: public administration workers struck on 20 November, with about 90% adhesion in schools by the unions' count, and CP train drivers struck from 23 to 27 November, with full stoppages on 24 and 25 November. Housing movements held a national day of action on 14 November; the CGTP marched in Lisbon on 21 November.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Wages; Public services; Cost of living; Housing.

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

Casa para Viver & Porta a Porta Since 10 October
Reverse the new lease law · No evictions without rehousing · Curb purchases by property funds · More public housing

CGTP (union confederation) Since 17 October
Higher wages and pensions · Right to health, housing and education · Lower and control essential prices

Frente Comum (public administration unions) Strike on 20 November
Pay rise of at least 15%, minimum €150 · Public-sector base pay of €1,150 · Negotiate the 83 proposals

SMAQ (train drivers' union) 23 November
Recover purchasing power · Better working conditions · Stronger rail safety

Journées de mobilisation

Train drivers' strike at CP (23-27 November)

lundi 23 novembre. Participation non communiquée

242 of 1,062 scheduled trains (22.8%) cancelled by 19:00 on the first day per CP. On 24 November, the first full-stoppage day, 795 of 1,062 trains (74.9%) were cancelled by 19:00 per CP; only the 267 minimum-service trains ran. On 26 November, a partial day, 225 of 1,063 trains (21.2%) were cancelled by 19:00 per CP data reported by Lusa. The strike ends on 27 November.

Sources : Público, 23 Nov 2026, Observador, 23 Nov 2026, Jornal Económico, 24 Nov 2026, RTP, 24 Nov 2026, DN / Lusa, 26 Nov 2026

CGTP national demonstration in Lisbon

samedi 21 novembre. Participation non communiquée

Several thousand marched in heavy rain from Saldanha to Restauradores (Lusa); 'tens of thousands' per CGTP. No official count.

Sources : Diário de Coimbra (Lusa), 21 Nov 2026, CGTP-IN, 21 Nov 2026

National public administration strike

vendredi 20 novembre. Participation non communiquée

About 90% in schools and 100% at some hospitals per Frente Comum; about 75% of doctors per FNAM. Schools closed in several districts, waste collection stopped in several municipalities, and monuments including the Jerónimos Monastery closed. No official national figure.

Sources : Público, 20 Nov 2026, Esquerda.net, 20 Nov 2026

National Day of Struggle for Housing

samedi 14 novembre. Participation non communiquée

Decentralised actions across the country, including Lisbon (Alameda) and Porto (Praça do Marquês) at 15:00, the Azores and Madeira.

Sources : RTP, Nov 2026

Second housing march

Housing march in Lisbon

CGTP national demonstrations in Lisbon, Porto and Coimbra
