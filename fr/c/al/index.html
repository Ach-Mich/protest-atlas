Manifestations en AlbanieAlbanie

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:49.

Student-led protests since 13 October, joined by the opposition Democratic Party, demand Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation over corruption, education costs and poor job prospects; clashes with police recur each week.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Corruption; Education costs; Jobs.

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

Students in Protest Since 13 October
Resignation of PM Edi Rama · Better university conditions and student support · Jobs for graduates

Democratic Party (opposition, Sali Berisha) Since 24 October
Resignation of PM Edi Rama · Release of detained protesters

Pression sur le gouvernement

Revendication
Démission. Demand PM Edi Rama's resignation
Réponse
Dialogue proposé. Government offered higher student grants and rent subsidies
Maintien de l'ordre
Dur. Rubber bullets and fines for joining violent protests

Sources : Al Jazeera, 17 Oct 2026, Business Day / Reuters, 25 Oct 2026, Al Jazeera, 29 Oct 2026
