Manifestations en CroatieCroatie

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:49.

School and university students boycotted classes and gathered in about a dozen cities on 20 November over living costs, housing, education and conscription. A civic initiative has called anti-corruption protests in several cities for 12 December.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Cost of living; Housing; Corruption; Education & health.

Prochaine mobilisation, samedi 12 décembre. The civic initiative "Vrijeme za promjene" has moved its anti-corruption protests in several cities (including Zagreb, Split, Rijeka, Osijek, Zadar and Pula) from 21 November to Saturday 12 December, citing safety and higher-than-expected interest; the youth strike organisers say they will continue.

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

Štrajk mladih u Hrvatskoj (Youth Strike in Croatia) 20 November
Lower living costs and affordable housing · Better education and healthcare · Demilitarisation, no conscription

Vrijeme za promjene (civic initiative) 12 December call
Fight corruption and abuse of power · Transparent public spending · Accountable institutions

Pression sur le gouvernement

Revendication
Changement de programme. Living costs, housing and services; anti-corruption protests called
Réponse
Pas encore de réponse. The education minister dismissed the strike as not spontaneous

Sources : Index.hr, 20 Nov 2026, N1 Hrvatska, 31 Oct 2026

Journées de mobilisation

Youth strike: class boycott and gatherings in about a dozen cities

vendredi 20 novembre. Participation non communiquée

Gatherings in Zagreb, Rijeka, Pula, Zadar and Osijek, with Split, Varaždin, Slavonski Brod and others on the organisers' list; turnout reported as uneven. The education ministry said morning absences were lower than a year earlier.
