Manifestations en SuisseSuisse

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:49.

Young people demonstrated in about ten cities on 20 November in a nationwide youth strike against rearmament and austerity, with about 40,000 taking part by the organisers' count; police used rubber bullets in Basel and Zurich. Earlier, climate and pro-Palestinian protests took place in September and October.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Military spending; Austerity; Education; Climate action.

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

Bündnis Jugendstreik (youth strike alliance) 20 November
Shift money from armament to education, social services and climate · 35-hour week · More holidays for apprentices · Three-franc meals at schools and universities
