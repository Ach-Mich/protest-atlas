Manifestations en ItalieItalie

Dernière vérification le vendredi 27 novembre à 06:49.

The CGIL union is mobilising against the Meloni government's budget law over wages, pensions and public services; its general strike on 20 November drew about 1.5 million people in more than 100 cities by the union's count. Grassroots unions strike again on 4 December.

Les résumés et titres viennent de nos sources et restent en anglais pour l'instant.

Pourquoi ils manifestent : Wages & pensions; Cost of living; Budget law; Military spending.

Prochaine mobilisation, vendredi 4 décembre. Grassroots unions (CUB, SGB, SI Cobas, Unicobas, ADL Varese, SBM, SGC) hold a 24-hour general strike across public and private sectors on Friday 4 December. The CGIL will join a health-sector demonstration in Rome on 5 December and holds a demonstration for the South in Cosenza on 12 December (Il Sole 24 Ore).

Qui manifeste, et ce qu'ils demandent

CGIL (union) Since 17 October
Higher wages and pensions · More for health, schools and housing · 1% wealth tax above €2 million · Less military spending

Grassroots unions (CUB, SGB, SI Cobas, Unicobas and others) 4 December strike call
Pay rises of at least 20% · Minimum wage of €12 an hour · Wage indexation to prices · Less military spending

Pression sur le gouvernement

Revendication
Changement de programme. CGIL and grassroots unions oppose the budget law

Pas encore de réponse

Journées de mobilisation

CGIL general strike with marches in more than 100 cities

vendredi 20 novembre. 1 500 000 organisateurs

About 1.5 million in more than 100 squares and 60% average strike adhesion per CGIL; about 20,000 students in Milan, about 7,000 in Genoa, 6,000-8,000 in Bari per organisers. Police used water cannon and tear gas in Turin; firecrackers thrown at the education ministry in Rome. PD leader Elly Schlein joined the march.

Sources : ANSA, 20 Nov 2026, Fanpage live, 20 Nov 2026

USB general strike with student marches in Milan, Rome and other cities

vendredi 30 octobre. Participation non communiquée

About 5,000 students in Milan and about 500 in Rome (Sapienza); marches also in Turin, Florence, Pisa and Chieti

Sources : Tecnica della Scuola, Newsistruzione, 30 Oct 2026

CGIL national demonstration in Rome

samedi 17 octobre. 100 000 organisateurs

More than 100,000 per CGIL; no official count. Landini announced a general strike for 20 November.

Sources : ANSA, 17 Oct 2026
