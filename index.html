How this works

Every morning we check news agencies, public broadcasters and official statements in 38 countries. A country goes on watch once a national protest or strike is reported. Turnout is always given twice, the official count and the organisers' count, with every source linked. Many protest days have no published figure at all, especially strikes and smaller marches: then the page says "Turnout not reported" rather than guess. "Very active" and pressure on government are our own readings, built with fixed rules, not official measures.
